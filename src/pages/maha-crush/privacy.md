This Privacy Policy explains how Maha Crush (“the Game”) collects, uses and protects information. The Game needs an internet connection to play. By using the Game, you agree to this policy.

## 1. Information we collect

Without signing in, the Game automatically collects:

- **Device information:** phone model, Android version, language, screen size and app version.
- **Identifiers:** a random app instance ID and your device’s advertising ID.
- **Game activity:** levels played, progress, purchases, rewards, time played and buttons used.
- **Approximate location:** country and region, estimated from your internet connection.
- **Crash and performance data:** technical details when the Game crashes or runs slowly.

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google Play Games or Firebase, we also collect your name, email address, user ID and saved game progress.

We do not collect your precise GPS location, contacts, photos or files.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:

- Run the Game and save your progress.
- Fix bugs and improve performance.
- Understand how the Game is played, using analytics.
- Show ads, including rewarded ads.
- Process in-app purchases.
- Respond to your support requests.

## 3. Third-party services

The Game uses these services, which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

- Google Firebase and Google Analytics, for analytics and crash reporting.
- Google Sign-In and Google Play Games, for optional sign-in and saved progress.
- Google Play Billing, for in-app purchases. We never see or store your card or payment details.
- Third-party advertising networks, for showing ads. They may use your advertising ID to show relevant ads.

## 4. Sign-in is optional

You can play without signing in. Signing in lets us save your profile and progress so you can restore it on another device.

## 5. Your choices and data deletion

- You can reset or opt out of personalised ads using your advertising ID settings in Android.
- You can stop using sign-in at any time.
- To delete your data, use the [Data Deletion Request page](/maha-crush/delete-data) on this website or email [bsgameslab@gmail.com](mailto:bsgameslab@gmail.com). We will delete your data within 30 days.

## 6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. Data security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted connections. We keep your data while your profile exists or until you ask us to delete it. Anonymous analytics data may be kept longer.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page with the updated date.
