You can ask us to delete the data linked to your Maha Crush account at any time.

## How to request deletion

- Send an email to [bsgameslab@gmail.com](mailto:bsgameslab@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20Maha%20Crush%20data) with the subject “Delete my Maha Crush data”.
- Write from the email address you used to sign in with Google. If you can, also include your player name from the Rank tab.
- We may reply to confirm that the request is from you.

## What we delete

- Your name, email address and user ID.
- Your saved progress, scores and leaderboard entries.
- Your profile and any other data linked to your account.

## What we may keep

- Anonymous analytics and crash data that cannot be linked to you.
- Purchase records that we must keep by law. These are held by Google Play.

## How long it takes

We delete your data within 30 days of confirming your request.

## Other things to know

- Deletion is permanent. Your progress cannot be restored afterwards.
- Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the Game.
- Google Play Games data is managed by Google. You can remove it in your Google Play Games settings.

## Questions?

Email [bsgameslab@gmail.com](mailto:bsgameslab@gmail.com).
