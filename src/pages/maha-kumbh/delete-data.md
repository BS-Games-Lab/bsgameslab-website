## How to request deletion

Email [bsgameslab@gmail.com](mailto:bsgameslab@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20data) with the subject "Delete my data". We will confirm and delete your data within 30 days.

## What gets deleted

All game activity data linked to your device (levels reached, buildings built, time played, buttons used), crash reports, and the app identifiers associated with your device.

## What is kept, and for how long

Purchase records are kept for up to 8 years, because Indian tax law requires it. Anonymous totals that cannot be linked back to any device, such as "1,000 players finished Level 2," are also kept.

## If you do not contact us

Game activity data is deleted automatically after 14 months of inactivity, and crash reports after 90 days. Uninstalling the game removes all progress stored on your phone.
