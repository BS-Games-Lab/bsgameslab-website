This Privacy Policy explains what information the Maha Kumbh Mela Game 3D app ("the Game") collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By playing the Game, you agree to this policy.

## 1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not require an account or login.

The Game automatically collects the following information:

- **Device information:** phone model, Android version, language, screen size and app version.
- **Identifiers:** a random ID created by the app (app instance ID) and your device's advertising ID.
- **Game activity:** levels reached, buildings built, upgrades, rewards collected, time played, and buttons used inside the Game.
- **Approximate location:** country and region, estimated from your internet connection. We do not use GPS.
- **Crash and performance data:** technical details when the Game crashes or runs slowly.
- **Purchase records:** what you bought inside the Game. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

## 2. Why we collect it

We use this information to:

- understand how players use the Game and where they get stuck, so we can improve it;
- fix crashes and bugs;
- balance game difficulty and rewards;
- deliver in-game purchases you have paid for;
- show ads, where the Game includes them (see Section 4).

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Third-party services we use

These services collect and process the information above on our behalf. Each has its own privacy policy:

- **Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config** – game usage, crash reports and game settings. [Firebase Privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Google Play Billing** – in-game purchases. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google AdMob** – advertising. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. Advertising

The Game shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, approximate location and basic device information to select ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and measure ad performance. Google's handling of this data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can limit personalized ads at any time: on most Android phones, go to **Settings → Google → Ads**, then reset or delete your advertising ID. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.

If you purchase the ad-free option inside the Game, ads are removed.

## 5. Sharing feature

The Game lets you create a status image with a quote and share it to apps such as WhatsApp or Facebook. Sharing happens through your phone's standard share menu. We do not access your contacts, messages or the apps you share to.

## 6. How long we keep data

Game activity data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. Your game progress is stored on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the Game.

## 7. Where data is stored

Our service providers (such as Google) may store and process data on servers outside India. They protect this data with industry-standard security.

## 8. Your choices and rights

- **Stop data collection:** uninstall the Game.
- **Limit ad personalization:** see Section 4.
- **Request deletion:** email us at [bsgameslab@gmail.com](mailto:bsgameslab@gmail.com) with the subject "Delete my data". We will respond within 30 days. See the [Data Deletion Request page](/maha-kumbh/delete-data).

## 9. Children

The Game is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game, contact us and we will delete any related data.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes, for example when ads are added. The "Last updated" date at the top will show when it was last changed.
